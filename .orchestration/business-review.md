# BOOK'D — business review

Reviewed 16 September 2026. Static, read-only assessment of the launch client and repository SQL; no live database queries, test writes, payment attempts, or commits. Courts and Shop are excluded. References are repository-relative `file:line`. SQL conclusions assume the hardening and subsequent migrations are applied; repository definitions and historical runbook statements do not establish today's live configuration.

**Commercial conclusion:** BOOK'D records prospective coaching turnover and commission, but has no implemented way to collect its cut or reconcile it to cash. Its only reachable transaction family is coach booking, including packages. Subscription, boost, advertising, promo, and loyalty structures do not constitute additional collected revenue. Direct coach payment is explicitly intentional for v1; the collection gap still matters if the business expects commission income. Sources: `README.md:126`, `expo-app/src/overlays/BookingOverlay.tsx:82`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:49`, `expo-app/src/state/sampleData.ts:28`.

**Inventory/counting:** 8 existing or intended commercial paths below; 8 REVENUE-CRITICAL findings and 7 OPERATIONAL gaps. Paths count single sessions and packages separately, and include dormant monetization and incentive mechanisms. None collects cash for BOOK'D end to end. Findings about concurrent calls are source-derived risks, not reproduced incidents.

## REVENUE-CRITICAL

### R1. Recorded commission has no route to BOOK'D's bank balance

**Reachable booking flow.** Confirmation writes `total_cents` and `commission_cents` before the session; even completion only changes `status`. There is no receipt, commission-paid state, coach debt balance, settlement reference, or reconciliation record in the booking schema or these mutations. Nothing in the inspected launch client collects the platform's share from the coach. Sources: `db/schema.sql:226`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:51`, `expo-app/src/overlays/CoachDayViewOverlay.tsx:77`.

For a hypothetical $50 session at 12%, the database records $6 of commission, while the client instructs the customer to hand the $50 to the coach. BOOK'D's $6 would have to be collected separately from the coach; the app neither records that transfer nor matches it against the snapshot. Revenue in the accounting UI is a literal initial zero, not a sum of commissions or receipts. Sources: `expo-app/src/overlays/BookingOverlay.tsx:61`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:54`, `expo-app/src/state/sampleData.ts:29`, `expo-app/src/state/store.ts:1149`.

**Deliberate v1 simplification, with a commercial consequence:** direct payment is explicitly designed, not a broken card charge (`README.md:126`, `SUBMISSION.md:104`). It is credible as a free marketplace launch. It does not support a claim that BOOK'D currently earns or collects the recorded take rate. Messaging also lets parties arrange sessions without making a booking, in which case the only identified commission producer never runs (`expo-app/src/overlays/PersonOverlay.tsx:18`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:51`).

### R2. Cancellation and no-show do not resolve package money or restore entitlement

**Reachable cancellation; API-supported no-show.** A package's first future reservation creates its balance immediately. Later reservations are priced at zero solely because that balance exists. Cancelling updates only `bookings.status`; it neither returns the consumed session nor changes the original amount/commission. Sources: `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:33`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:57`, `expo-app/src/lib/bookings.ts:193`, `db/hardening.sql:325`.

Example derived from that flow: reserve the first session of a five-session $200 pack, cancel it before paying, then book another session. The cancelled record still contains $200 plus commission, `used` remains 1, and the new record is $0; the confirmation can say there is “nothing to pay.” Conversely, someone who really paid loses a reserved session when cancelling, regardless of notice or whose fault it was. Sources: `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:37`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:64`, `expo-app/src/overlays/BookingOverlay.tsx:58`.

The same status guard accepts a coach's `no_show` or `cancelled` update without financial consequences. There is no distinction between coach and client no-show, cancellation reason, fee, refund, or credit in the booking row. Booking notifications run **after insert only**, so cancellation also generates no counterpart notification through that trigger. Sources: `db/hardening.sql:337`, `db/schema.sql:23`, `db/schema.sql:226`, `db/migrations/2026-09-15_booking_notifications.sql:47`.

**Defect/undefined commercial policy, not merely deferred payments:** preserving historical prices is sensible, but there is no adjustment record establishing what remains owed after cancellation. An external refund cannot be reconciled here. There is also no disclosed forfeiture rule in the actual cancellation prompt (`expo-app/src/overlays/BookingsOverlay.tsx:197`).

### R3. Concurrent package bookings can record duplicate purchases or excess free sessions

**Authenticated booking API; concurrent screens/devices suffice.** The RPC reads `used,total` without locking, decides purchase versus redemption, inserts a booking, then upserts the balance. The unique balance index prevents duplicate balance rows; it does not serialize the earlier decision. Sources: `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:33`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:51`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:58`, `db/hardening.sql:582`.

Two first bookings at different times can both see no balance and each record the full package price and commission. With one session left, two calls can both authorize zero-price redemptions; the later upsert caps `used` with `least(...)`, masking the extra booking. The slot index only prevents the same coach/timestamp among pending/confirmed bookings, not different timestamps competing for one package. Sources: `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:37`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:64`, `db/hardening.sql:566`.

**Defect, source-derived concurrency risk.** The sequential package invariant exercises only two ordinary calls, so it does not establish protection against this interleaving (`db/tests/release_invariants.sql:85`, `db/tests/release_invariants.sql:92`). These are duplicate recorded liabilities or excessive entitlements, not duplicate processor debits.

### R4. Changing the public package changes existing buyers' rights

**Reachable coach package editor.** Each stepper immediately writes new sessions/price to the same package ID; “Stop selling” sets `active=false`. Redemption first requires that same package still be active, and then replaces the purchased balance's `total` with the package's current session count. Sources: `expo-app/src/overlays/CoachOverlays.tsx:235`, `expo-app/src/overlays/CoachOverlays.tsx:258`, `expo-app/src/overlays/CoachOverlays.tsx:854`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:28`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:62`.

Consequences: retiring an offer strands remaining sessions; lowering its session count shrinks an existing buyer's entitlement on a later redemption; raising it can grant extra sessions without a new purchase or commission. The original booking price remains historical, but the purchased service quantity is not stable. Sources: `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:39`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:59`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:63`.

The schema's `expires_on` is also inert for enforcement: the RPC neither sets nor tests it, while balance cards display an expiry if one exists. Therefore even a manually dated entitlement can still redeem after that date. Sources: `db/schema.sql:251`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:33`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:58`, `expo-app/src/overlays/BookingsOverlay.tsx:142`. **Defect for sold packages; no evidence that changing already purchased terms was an intended v1 bargain.**

### R5. A satisfied customer cannot repurchase the same exhausted package

**Normal client UI and server refusal.** One balance exists per client/coach/package, and `used >= total` raises an error permanently for that package ID. The screen disables confirmation with “Pack already used”; there is no new-purchase cycle in the RPC. Sources: `db/hardening.sql:582`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:37`, `expo-app/src/overlays/BookingOverlay.tsx:83`, `expo-app/src/overlays/BookingOverlay.tsx:91`.

This affects one-session packages too. If any active packages exist, the client returns only those options and omits the fallback unbundled session. A coach with one exhausted offer can therefore become unbookable to a repeat customer through normal selection. Creating a different package ID is a different offer, not an implemented repurchase flow. Sources: `expo-app/src/state/models.ts:200`, `expo-app/src/overlays/CoachOverlays.tsx:246`. **Repeat-revenue defect**, beyond the intentional rule that one pack should not be redeemed without limit.

### R6. Negotiated prices can produce zero commission; negative rates are accepted by the schema

**Zero-price flow is reachable; negative-price path is API-level.** A missing coach rate defaults to zero, and the UI explicitly tells the customer to agree the price directly. The RPC records that zero as the total and computes zero commission. There is no later agreed-price write: the client only has a status update grant on bookings. Sources: `db/schema.sql:123`, `expo-app/src/state/models.ts:212`, `expo-app/src/overlays/BookingOverlay.tsx:89`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:45`, `db/hardening.sql:315`.

If an offline price is subsequently agreed, that commercial value never reaches the commission snapshot. Zero-price packages are also permitted by the database and existing-package decrement control, so the same distinction matters between intentionally free service and service priced outside the system. Sources: `db/schema.sql:189`, `expo-app/src/overlays/CoachOverlays.tsx:854`.

Unlike packages, `coach_profiles.price_cents` has no nonnegative constraint. The owner can write that field through the authenticated API; the RPC copies it without a floor, and bookings have no nonnegative money checks. A negative rate can therefore create negative turnover and commission records. No negative-rate editor was found in the launch UI, and this does not represent a real cash refund. Sources: `db/schema.sql:117`, `db/schema.sql:234`, `db/hardening.sql:197`, `db/hardening.sql:203`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:45`.

**Mixed intent:** “agree directly” is explicit v1 behavior; loss of that price from a commission-based business is its unresolved cost. Negative financial records are a validation defect.

### R7. The amount shown after confirmation is not the server's recorded amount

**Reachable normal booking flow.** The screen computes its quote from cached profile/package data and a separately fetched usage map. Usage starts empty and errors return an empty map, both interpreted as a first purchase. The confirm button is not held for that lookup. Sources: `expo-app/src/overlays/BookingOverlay.tsx:28`, `expo-app/src/overlays/BookingOverlay.tsx:35`, `expo-app/src/overlays/BookingOverlay.tsx:91`, `expo-app/src/lib/queries.ts:367`.

The server independently prices the booking, returns only an ID, and the store discards even that ID before displaying success. The confirmation continues to use the locally computed `dueNow`. Thus an existing pack holder can be instructed to pay the full price while the saved booking is zero; a coach's intervening price edit can also leave the shown quote different from the saved amount. Sources: `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:28`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:67`, `expo-app/src/state/store.ts:993`, `expo-app/src/overlays/BookingOverlay.tsx:57`.

Discovery/profile prices are independently based on `coach_profiles.price_cents`, while package-only booking may require a different total. That discrepancy is visible before confirmation but is another reason the advertised “Book a session” amount is not necessarily the selected purchase price. Sources: `expo-app/src/lib/queries.ts:96`, `expo-app/src/overlays/PersonOverlay.tsx:49`, `expo-app/src/state/models.ts:211`. **Pricing/trust defect:** in a direct-pay business, an incorrect payment instruction can cause a real overpayment even without a processor.

### R8. Subscription gating provides permanent free access and does not gate sales

**Schema/API entitlement, reachable tools once a coach profile exists.** Non-admin coach-profile creation forces active status and a null period end. The role resolver treats null as unexpired. The release test explicitly expects an ordinary authenticated account to become COACH by inserting a profile, without payment. Sources: `db/hardening.sql:205`, `db/hardening.sql:214`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_account_role.sql:41`, `db/tests/release_invariants.sql:180`.

Even manually setting an overdue/expired subscription would primarily remove the normal coach menu: discovery does not filter subscription status, and booking does not check it. Package/availability/promo ownership policies also do not test an active subscription. Sources: `expo-app/src/screens/ProfileScreen.tsx:89`, `expo-app/src/lib/queries.ts:119`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:27`, `db/policies.sql:47`.

**Conditional commercial risk, explicitly acknowledged v1 decision:** the runbook says free coaching may be correct (`SUBMISSION.md:127`). This is not proven theft of a priced subscription; there is no subscription price to collect. If recurring coach fees underpin the business plan, however, there is neither monetization nor an effective service-expiry gate. Normal users cannot currently create a coach profile through the observed signup flow; that separate acquisition gap is O3, not evidence that the free entitlement is unreachable through the API.

## OPERATIONAL

### O1. A disputed payment or coach no-show has no financial case-management tool

A client can cancel an upcoming booking or report a person; there is no refund, reschedule, coach-no-show claim, balance correction, or booking-linked dispute action on the booking card. Past sessions have no cancellation callback. A coach's day screen only offers Mark done. Sources: `expo-app/src/overlays/BookingsOverlay.tsx:106`, `expo-app/src/overlays/BookingsOverlay.tsx:123`, `expo-app/src/overlays/BookingsOverlay.tsx:197`, `expo-app/src/overlays/CoachDayViewOverlay.tsx:207`, `expo-app/src/overlays/PersonOverlay.tsx:123`.

The server permits coaches to set cancelled/no_show/completed/confirmed, but does not restrict their old status or require the session to have started. Those stricter completion conditions exist only in the UI. Clients cannot mark no-show, and an unrelated platform admin has no booking-read/status override under the inspected policies/guard. Sources: `db/hardening.sql:311`, `db/hardening.sql:332`, `expo-app/src/overlays/CoachDayViewOverlay.tsx:89`.

**First incident:** the customer can message the coach or file a generic profile report, but BOOK'D staff cannot investigate a complete payment trail, return money, or repair the pack through the app. Report submission stores reason/summary only, without a booking reference or evidence snapshot; the runbook also says no in-app support route exists. Sources: `expo-app/src/overlays/PersonOverlay.tsx:18`, `expo-app/src/lib/notifications.ts:89`, `expo-app/src/lib/notifications.ts:107`, `SUBMISSION.md:116`. External cash refunds are consistent with direct payment; the lack of intervention and reconciliation remains an operational gap.

### O3. Provider onboarding and departure need outside administration

Choosing Coach/Teacher records intent and changes discovery mode; email signup sends name/email/password, and the signup trigger creates only a user row. No launch client creation/update path for a coach profile or its base rate was found; existing coach tools edit packages and availability instead. Consequently a newly signed-up coach cannot reach the role-gated tools until a profile is provisioned outside this normal flow. Departure is problematic too. Following the reachable Delete account call, the edge function removes credentials and anonymizes the user but retains coach profiles, packages, bookings, and balances. Discovery does not exclude deleted users, and booking checks neither deletion nor provider availability. A deleted coach can therefore remain listed/bookable as “Deleted account,” with nobody able to deliver or manage those sessions. ### O5. Platform approval screens do not process real incoming requests

## NOTED

### N1. Dormant boosts, promos, and loyalty are not present-day collected revenue or unlimited-credit exploits

Boost dates/fees are disconnected from the discovery boolean. If that boolean is manually granted, its benefit has no inspected time limit; ordinary users cannot self-set it. This is latent commercialization risk, not proof that anyone purchased or obtained a free boost in production. Sources: `db/schema.sql:173`, `expo-app/src/lib/queries.ts:97`, `db/hardening.sql:220`, `db/hardening.sql:228`.

Their lack of usage limits, monetary conversion, or redemption idempotency therefore does **not** establish an active double-discount exploit. The reachable management UIs expressly label redemption/earning as deferred; this is a deliberate v1 omission. ## Unit economics and day-one operating reality

**Applied formula:** `round(total_cents × coalesce(platform_margins['session'].pct, 12) / 100)`. The session row is authoritative, including a legitimate configured 0%; 12% is the fallback if no row is found. The development seed also specifies 12%, but is explicitly opt-in demo data, so it is not evidence of today's live percentage. Sources: `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:49`, `db/seed.sql:10`, `db/seed.sql:273`. The live rate was not queried for this read-only repository review.

| Example at the 12% fallback | Recorded customer total | Recorded BOOK'D commission | What cash collection is implemented |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| One $50 session | $50 | $6 | Customer is instructed to pay coach; BOOK'D collection absent. |
| First reservation of a $200 five-session package | $200 | $24 | Same direct-pay instruction; no receipt validation. |
| Each later redemption in that package | $0 | $0 | UI says already covered; based on usage, not a receipt. |
| A session with zero stored price, then negotiated offline | $0 | $0 | Agreed amount never enters this record. |

Examples are arithmetic from `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:37` and `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:54`, with payment language from `expo-app/src/overlays/BookingOverlay.tsx:57`. If all five $200-pack sessions are delivered, its recorded commission averages $4.80 per session; neither that nor the $6 single-session figure is established collected revenue. Currency is displayed as `$`, while booking money has no currency field; no multi-currency conversion or tax line is represented in the inspected quote/schema (`expo-app/src/overlays/BookingOverlay.tsx:39`, `db/schema.sql:226`).

**Can the rate change without an app deploy? Yes at the database layer; no through the current app console.** The existing SQL supports an admin-created proposal and three distinct `is_admin` approvals, after which a trigger updates existing margin rows, writes history, and notifies users. This affects new booking snapshots; it does not reprice old bookings or collect anything. Sources: `db/hardening.sql:369`, `db/hardening.sql:376`, `db/hardening.sql:399`, `db/hardening.sql:424`, `db/hardening.sql:436`, `db/auth.sql:355`, `db/migrations/2026-09-03_package_redemption.sql:49`. Operating that existing backend workflow requires database/API access outside the disconnected client accounting controls described in O4.

**Who can run day one:** already provisioned coaches can manage packages, timetable, and completion; backend-designated admins can triage reports and maintain promo/reward configuration; community owners can create/manage limited content when their role is known to the client. Collection, disputes/refunds, account enforcement, provider provisioning, real approval queues, and financial reconciliation require work outside the shipped app. This division follows the actual reachable menus and mutations, not the mere presence of admin tables (`expo-app/src/screens/ProfileScreen.tsx:88`, `expo-app/src/screens/ProfileScreen.tsx:148`, `expo-app/src/overlays/CommunityOverlays.tsx:38`, `expo-app/src/lib/moderation.ts:240`, `expo-app/src/overlays/AdminOverlays.tsx:74`, `expo-app/src/overlays/AdminOverlays.tsx:107`).
